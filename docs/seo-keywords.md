# Rivelo search topics

These are intended content targets, not measured search volumes or confirmed rankings. Each page answers a different visitor need; related phrases belong in useful copy, headings, titles and relevant internal links.

| Page | Main topic | Related search phrases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | App and website directory | discover new apps, indie apps, startup discovery, independent products, free product directory |
| `/tools/visibility-check` | Website analyzer and SEO checker | website SEO audit, landing page analyzer, meta tag checker, social preview checker, sitemap checks, technical SEO issues |
| `/app-growth` | App growth | app marketing, free app promotion, get first users, SaaS growth, landing page improvements, startup growth strategy |
| `/launch-guide` | Startup launch platforms | Product Hunt alternatives, free launch sites, startup directories, SaaS directories, where to launch an app |
| `/premium` | App and website promotion | app growth plans, product promotion, sponsored product listings, website growth plans |
| `/register` | Free product submission | submit your app, list your SaaS, submit a startup, free app listing, website submission |

The website analyzer performs a single-page technical review. Keep its copy clear about the one-free-check allowance and avoid claims about a full-site crawl, app store rankings or guaranteed search visibility. The private Growth dashboard stays separate from the public app growth guide.

After publishing, use Search Console's queries and landing pages to see which topics bring impressions and engaged visitors. Update pages based on that evidence. Meta-keyword tags are not part of this strategy.
